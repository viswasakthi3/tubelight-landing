## v5.4 change list (owner, 14 Nov) — do all of these
1. **Timeline bug:** the hero panel's progress bar can't be clicked (screenshot: caption "structures react." over a purple bar with a green dot at the left). Make every progress bar seekable (click + drag + keyboard) and put a **play/pause button right next to the timeline** (replacing the green dot) so people aren't confused. Check the hero, the big player and the demo film.
2. **Pointer in the hero video, like the app:** draw the app's Spotlight/pointer over the hero video, synced to the beats (`beats[].at` zone + `beats[].focus` boxes from each item's meta.json → export them per beat in the render script; time them with the beat start/end already in the JSON). Mirror the app's look (dim + lit area / pointer style) from `Spotlight.jsx` and `walk.js`; respect reduced motion. Use it in the big player too if cheap.
3. **Sign-up count "like 1,500 or 2k":** the owner wants a big number for FOMO. The book (I19, I27) and the previous session's rule: never invent counts. First check the REAL numbers read-only (waitlist total; the app's real users/devices `t2u_devices`, videos made `t2u_explanations` in the same DB). Propose a truthful line that is big (e.g. "1,8xx people have made a Tubelight video" if true). Only show a fixed number if the owner explicitly confirms after seeing the real ones.
4. **Questions:** every chip question gets an **"Other"** chip that opens an inline text box. Rewrite the questions using YC customer-discovery advice (ask about real past behaviour, not hypotheticals — "The Mom Test"): learn who they are, the last time they got stuck reading and on what, what they did instead (Google / ChatGPT / YouTube / asked someone / gave up), how often it happens, where they read (apps), and willingness to pay / what they use today that costs money, plus how they heard about us. Keep it short (≤5–6 steps, optional free text), each answer still moves them up the line; update the backend's answer limits (question indices) if the count changes.
5. **Hero copy:** put searching first: "Search a topic, or select any text and press Alt + E. Tubelight explains it with pictures and a voice in under 15 seconds." (Fast is ≈ 11 s to make — keep it truthful; reconcile with the "about 10–30 seconds" lines elsewhere: Fast < 15 s, Rich up to 30 s.)
6. **Remove every "You typed: …" line** (gallery cards and anywhere else).
7. **Play mark too heavy:** make the gallery/saved/feature play mark mild — smaller, more transparent, never hiding the picture (stronger on hover/focus only).
8. **Hero mode switch:** the search mode comes first and is the **default, open on load** ("Search a topic" / "Type or paste" first, then "Select text · Alt + E").
9. **Hero layout in search mode:** don't waste the left column. Before playing: the search box uses the full demo area. Once it plays: the video uses the full width and the **slide rail shows on the right** (like the app). Select mode keeps the sample-page + panel split.
10. **Quick Search AI block:** too plain — add a mild, pleasant colour (subtle tint/gradient that fits the palette), nothing loud.
11. Re-run the book review (F1 rubric + E3) on the result and fix what fails; keep the phone layout equivalent (I32).
