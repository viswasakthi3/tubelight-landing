Questions, answered.

What is Tubelight?

A Windows app that explains anything you select. Select text, press Alt + E, and you get a short explanation — pictures, a voice and captions — right beside what you’re reading.

When will I get access?

We invite people in batches, in waitlist order. People who tell us how they’ll use Tubelight move up the line, so they’re invited sooner.

Can I try it before I’m invited?

Yes. Type a topic in the search box under “Curious about anything?” and Tubelight makes a free preview video right on this page — one a day. You’ll need your email to watch it, which also puts you on the waitlist.

What happens after I join?

Nothing, until your invite is ready. Then we email you a download link. No newsletters, and you can ask us to remove your email at any time.

Why not just ask ChatGPT?

You can — but that means switching tabs, typing a question and reading a wall of text. Tubelight shows and tells you, in seconds, without leaving your page.

Does it cost anything?

It’s free to try, with no card needed. Paid plans are there for people who learn a lot every day.

Is it accurate?

It’s very good on school, college and everyday topics. But like any AI it can slip now and then. If something looks off, ask a follow-up — and for anything high-stakes, check your textbook or teacher.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows 10 and 11. A Mac version is on the way — join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when it’s ready.

What happens to the text I select?

It’s sent to our servers to make your explanation. We keep a record of the explanations we make so we can fix problems and improve them. Your saved explanations also live on your own PC.
