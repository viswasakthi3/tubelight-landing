# Search demo photographs

These local WebP assets replace the illustrated Moon and cherry blossoms in the answer cards and sample-question previews.

## pic-moon.webp

- **Image:** The Near Side of the Moon, a photographic mosaic from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter cameras.
- **Credit:** NASA / GSFC / Arizona State University.
- **Source:** https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-near-side-of-the-moon/
- **Original:** https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/psd/lunar-science/2023/08/lro_fullmoon.jpg
- **Processing:** Resized to 800 × 800 and encoded as WebP. The full lunar disc is preserved.

## pic-blossom.webp

- **Image:** Pink cherry blossoms on a tree branch, photographed in Ueno, Tokyo.
- **Photographer:** ayumi kubo.
- **Source:** https://unsplash.com/photos/pink-cherry-blossoms-on-a-tree-branch-HQJoZCyNcbg
- **Original:** https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1775315877321-9d69fc83af40
- **License:** Unsplash License — https://unsplash.com/license
- **Processing:** Cropped to a 1200 × 600 landscape image and encoded as WebP.

The photographs are served locally; the page does not depend on external image hosts at runtime.
