# Quick Search AI

> Quick Search AI is a native Windows desktop voice and screen search application developed by Tubelight. It allows users to hold Tab + Q anywhere across Windows to ask questions out loud, highlight words for instant explanation, or snip screen areas with Tab + S to receive concise, multi-source web answers with citations and audio narration without opening browser tabs.

## Core Capabilities
- **Voice Search (Hold Tab + Q)**: Press and hold Tab + Q in any active window (browsers, IDEs, PDFs, games, office apps). Speak naturally, then release the keys. Quick Search answers instantly in a corner popup.
- **Contextual Text Lookup (Select + Tab + Q)**: Highlight unfamiliar words, phrases, or code snippets and press Tab + Q to get immediate visual definitions and explanations without switching tabs.
- **Screen Snip & OCR Search (Tab + S)**: Box any region of your display (error dialogs, charts, diagrams) to ask questions about it or extract text via local on-device OCR.
- **Sourced Answers with Citations**: Answers synthesize real-time web results with direct source chips (e.g. Wikipedia, NASA, official docs) and photographic evidence.
- **Text-to-Speech Narration**: Answers are automatically or optionally read aloud with synchronized text-to-speech.

## Free Allowance & Pricing
- **Daily Quota**: 50 free searches per device every 24 hours. Quota refreshes automatically at midnight.
- **Zero Friction**: No account, no email sign-up, no credit card required.
- **Local OCR**: Copying text out of screen captures with Tab + S runs locally on your PC and does not consume search quota.

## System Requirements & Download
- **Operating System**: Windows 10 (build 19041+) and Windows 11 (64-bit).
- **Distribution**: Available free on the official Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NFK7MS99FB3

## Privacy & Security Architecture
- **Microphone**: Listens only while holding Tab + Q; stops immediately upon key release. No background ambient listening.
- **History**: Search history is stored exclusively on the user's local PC and can be cleared at any time in Settings.
- **Tab Key**: Normal Tab key behavior, Alt+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, and Shift+Tab are completely unaffected. Quick Search activates only when Tab is held simultaneously with Q or S.
